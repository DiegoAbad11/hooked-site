Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Hooked (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an offline-first fishing logbook. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: your logbook never leaves your device, we have no servers, and we have no account system.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Everything you log is stored locally on your device.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
  • The only data that ever leaves your device is approximate GPS coordinates, sent to weather and tide providers so the app can show conditions for your spot.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following lives in a local database on your phone and is never sent to us (we operate no backend):

  • Sessions and catches (species, length, weight, notes, date/time)
  • Photos you attach to a catch
  • GPS coordinates and manual locations for your catches and map pins
  • Captured weather, tide, and moon-phase data for each session
  • Gear profiles (rod, reel, line, tackle)
  • Your preferences (units, language, region)

You can export this data to a file you control, and import it on another device, at any time from Settings → Backup. These files are created and shared only when you choose to; we never receive them.

Data sent to third parties

To show conditions, the app may send your approximate location coordinates to the following providers. No name, email, account, or device identifier is sent — only the coordinates needed to return weather or tide data:

  • OpenWeatherMap — current weather, when you have a session with location. Privacy policy.
  • WorldTides — tide state for saltwater locations only. Privacy policy.

These lookups are optional and non-blocking: if you are offline, deny location, or no API key is configured, the app simply omits weather/tide data and keeps working. Moon phase is always calculated on your device with no network call.

We do not control these providers’ handling of request data. Please review their policies. We do not send them anything that identifies you.

Device permissions

  • Location (When In Use) — to stamp where a catch was logged and pin it on your private map, and to fetch local weather/tides. Denying it disables those conveniences; you can still log catches and enter locations manually.
  • Camera — to photograph a catch, if you choose to add a photo.
  • Photo Library — to attach an existing photo to a catch.

Photos and locations are stored locally with your logbook and are not uploaded.

Children’s privacy

Hooked is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected by us, there is nothing for us to collect from a child.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control. Deleting a catch, session, or the app itself removes the associated data from your device. We hold no copy and therefore cannot retain or delete data on your behalf — there is none on our side.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the revised version at the same URL.
